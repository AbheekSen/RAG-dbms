Acme Corp Leave Policy

Annual Leave: Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried forward to the next year.

Sick Leave: Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

Remote Work: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
